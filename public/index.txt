# PretendPro Office Suite — Set Up Your Fake Workday

> A suite of fake-content demo tools — fake code editors, inboxes, documents,
> onboarding flows, and social mockups — for making realistic product
> screenshots and demos. Everything on the site is fictional parody.

## What it is

PretendPro Office Suite ([pretend.pro](https://pretend.pro)) is a wholesome
parody office suite by Mike "Demo" Demopoulos. It boots a pretend operating
system full of useless-but-convincing fake apps, so you can take screenshots
and record demos that look like real work without using any real data.
Everything on the site is fictional: imaginary coworkers, nonsense documents,
stock-photo slides.

Answer two questions on the homepage — which pretend work you want to mimic
and which window style feels most like your job — and your fake workday boots
in a full-screen pretend desktop or phone.

## The eleven demo tools

- [DocuFaker](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/docufaker) — fake document editor
- [SheetShenanigans](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/sheets) — fake spreadsheet
- [BrowserBuddy](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/browser) — fake web browser
- [Inbox Mirage](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/inbox) — fake email inbox
- [CodeFaker](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/codeweb) — fake web code editor
- [CodeFaker: Game](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/codegame) — fake game-dev editor
- [DeckDreamer](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/deck) — fake slide-deck builder
- [ReaderRealm](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/reader) — fake PDF reader
- [PhotoPretender](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/photos) — fake photo editor
- [ReelPretender](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/reels) — fake video editor
- [SoundStage](https://pretend.pro/us-en/fruit/sound) — fake audio editor

All eleven tools ship in every edition and every locale. Swap the locale
(`us-en`, `ca-en`, `uk-en`, `au-en`, `at-en`, `tlh`) and edition (`fruit`,
`apperture`, `bufferium`, `android`, `fos`) in the URL.
