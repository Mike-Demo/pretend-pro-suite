# Pricing

PretendPro Office Suite is **free**. There are no tiers, no trials, no paid
plans, and no enterprise upsell — because there is nothing to sell. It is a
parody side project that runs entirely in your browser.

- Cost to use: $0
- Accounts required: none
- Features gated behind payment: none exist

If anyone ever asks you to pay for PretendPro, that is not us.
