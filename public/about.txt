# About PretendPro Office Suite

PretendPro Office Suite is a wholesome parody office suite: a collection of
browser-based fake-content demo tools for making realistic product screenshots
and demos. It was built by Mike "Demo" Demopoulos, a partnerships leader in
cloud infrastructure and hosting who also builds playful AI workflow tooling.

## Why it exists

Good product demos need realistic-looking screens, but real screens contain
real data — real inboxes, real documents, real code. PretendPro solves this by
being convincingly fake on purpose: imaginary coworkers with imaginary
deadlines, nonsense documents typed with total conviction, stock-photo slides
presented with fake confidence. Nothing on the site is real, so nothing can
leak.

## What you get

- **Eleven parody apps**: DocuFaker, SheetShenanigans, BrowserBuddy, Inbox
  Mirage, CodeFaker, CodeFaker: Game, DeckDreamer, ReaderRealm, PhotoPretender,
  ReelPretender, and SoundStage.
- **Five pretend operating systems**: Fruit (Mac OS X style), Apperture
  (Windows style), BufferiumOS, Android, and fOS.
- **Six locales**: US, Canadian, UK, Austrian, and Australian English — plus
  tlhIngan Hol (Klingon), because why not.
- **Zero cost, zero accounts**: everything runs in your browser. Free forever.
