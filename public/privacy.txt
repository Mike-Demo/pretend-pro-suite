# Privacy

PretendPro Office Suite is a static parody website. It has no accounts, no
sign-in, no analytics dashboards, and no backend database.

## What is collected

- **Essentially nothing.** The site does not ask for your name, email, or any
  personal information.
- Standard web-server logs (IP address, requested page, timestamp) may exist
  briefly at the hosting layer, as with any website.

## What is not collected

- No cookies for tracking. No advertising pixels. No third-party analytics
  that follow you across the web.
- Everything you type into the fake tools stays in your browser — there is no
  server to send it to.
